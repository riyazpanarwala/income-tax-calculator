### Presentation differences

- ITD combines rebate and rebate marginal relief under “Rebate under section 156”. This app shows separate rows.
- ITD's “Surcharge” output is after surcharge marginal relief. This app preserves gross surcharge and relief separately.
- At the three threshold-plus-₹10 examples, exact cess ends in ₹0.40. ITD displays whole rupees; this app retains the paise in the breakdown before statutory final rounding. The final liabilities agree.
- The official simple calculator does not expose separate special-rate gains, salary components or property losses. **There is no claim that these components were validated against live official special-rate results.** Their tests are statute-derived fixtures and explicit exclusions.

## Explicit release limits

Do not market this as an unrestricted tax filing calculator. Salary exemptions, capital/business losses, foreign/treaty relief, agricultural integration, other special incomes, unclassified LTCG, grandfathering, protected property indexation, ambiguous exemption allocation, and mixed-income surcharge cases are not implemented. The UI and engine withhold estimates when these cases are indicated. A user supplying incorrectly precomputed business profits, capital gains, annual value or perquisites is outside the verified input contract.

These exclusions are deliberate correctness boundaries permitted by the specification. Supporting them requires additional input models, statutory review and independent representative fixtures before the guards can be removed.
